Features

Get started

Check whether someone is logged in, send them through Authwave and bring them back to your application. Small, open-source client libraries keep the integration easy to follow.

Give your login screens a familiar feel with your own branding, colours and login domain. Keep the authentication experience consistent with the application your users came to use.

Passwords are hashed before storage, and Authwave handles email verification, login sessions and account recovery. You get a consistent authentication flow without rebuilding those pieces for every project.

Read the code, understand how it works and change what you need. The provider and client libraries are MIT licensed, with the freedom to self-host and use them commercially.
