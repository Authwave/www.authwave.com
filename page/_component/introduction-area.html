Introduction

Add auth when you're ready

Authwave lets you build your application first, without thinking about authentication. When your app works, you can add Authwave in under a minute with only a few lines of code. It drops in cleanly and stays out of your way.

Open source and simple to run

Authwave is open source and independently built. You can self-host it at no cost, or use the hosted service for £5 per project per month. Every account includes three projects free.

One straightforward user flow

Authwave uses a single unified flow for sign up, log in, and recovery. Users enter their email and choose a password or a magic link. If they change or forget a password, the flow adapts automatically. There are no extra forms and no extra steps to manage.

A clear dashboard for managing your projects

The Authwave dashboard is where you can view usage analytics, review or ban users, download integration snippets, and adjust your project settings. Everything is in one place and easy to reach.
